THE FOUNDATION

An environment around agents—not another closed agent.

01

Agent-agnostic

Use the coding agents you already trust. Axio gives them a consistent workspace without pretending they are all the same.

02

Local-first

Your repositories, terminals, worktrees, and session state remain under your control. An account is not the price of entry.

03

Rust reliability

Process supervision, persistence, protocols, and permissions belong in a small, testable native core.

ONE CONTINUOUS FLOW

Open. Delegate.
Review. Continue.

Axio is designed around the real loop of agent-assisted development, including the parts between prompts.

  1. 01
    Open a repository

    Restore projects, worktrees, terminals, and context.

  2. 02
    Launch the right agents

    Work in one checkout or isolate tasks deliberately.

  3. 03
    Stay above the work

    See progress, questions, commands, and failures together.

  4. 04
    Review the result

    Compare approaches, annotate diffs, and merge with confidence.

PUBLIC FROM THE FIRST COMMIT

Help shape the workspace.

Axio begins at 0.0.1 and will adapt to what the product teaches us. There is no artificial intermediate version schedule. The destination is a dependable 1.0 agent workspace.
